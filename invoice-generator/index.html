⚠️ About these tax rates — accuracy notes
  • US rates are state-level only. Local county/city sales tax is not included, so the real combined rate at checkout is often higher.
  • US exemptions are treated as flat 0%. A few states tax certain goods (e.g. groceries) at a genuine nonzero reduced rate rather than fully exempting them — this tool doesn't model that.
  • EU reduced rates show only the primary tier. Several countries have two reduced rates; only the most common one is included here.
  • Rates are a snapshot, not live data. VAT/GST/sales tax rates change periodically and may not reflect the current law.
  • No ID/jurisdiction validation. VAT number formats and ZIP-to-state mapping aren't checked — reverse charge relies on you confirming both parties' VAT IDs are valid.

This is a self-serve estimate, not a tax-compliance service. For high-volume or audit-sensitive invoicing, verify rates with a tax API (e.g. Avalara, TaxJar, Stripe Tax) or an accountant.
